# Winning Demo Script

## 90-Second Story

UrbanFix AI turns citizen reports into prioritized, trackable municipal action.

1. Open the citizen portal.
2. Upload or select a civic issue image.
3. Add a short description and area.
4. Submit the complaint.
5. Show the authority dashboard updating with a priority score and department.
6. Run the AI demo to show classification, severity, summary, and governance insight.
7. Send a Civic Works contractor offer for a small repair.
8. Open tracking and show the citizen can see the issue status.

## Judge Questions To Preempt

- This is not just a complaint portal. It is a decision-support platform.
- AI interprets messy citizen input; deterministic scoring prioritizes action.
- MongoDB stores complaint, status, contractor, offer, and proof history.
- Contractor work is controlled by government approval, budget caps, verification, and proof.
- Citizen validation helps prevent fake closure.

## Strong One-Liner

UrbanFix AI turns a citizen's photo and location into prioritized, trackable government action.
